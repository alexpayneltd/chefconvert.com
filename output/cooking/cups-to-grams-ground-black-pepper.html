Common questions

How many Grams are in a cup of Ground Black Pepper?

One cup of Ground Black Pepper is approximately 100 Grams, based on standard density measurements.

Does the conversion change if the Ground Black Pepper is melted or solid?

Freshly cracked pepper is coarser and less dense than pre-ground — this conversion assumes standard fine-ground black pepper.

Is this conversion the same in every country?

Cup measurements vary slightly between US, UK, and metric cup standards. This converter uses the US standard cup (240ml).
