How many Grams are in a tsp of Ground Ginger?

One tsp of Ground Ginger is approximately 2.1 Grams, based on standard density measurements.

Does the conversion change if the Ground Ginger is melted or solid?

Ground ginger settles over time in storage — give the jar a shake before spooning to loosen it for a more accurate scoop.

Is this conversion the same in every country?

Cup measurements vary slightly between US, UK, and metric cup standards. This converter uses the US standard cup (240ml).
