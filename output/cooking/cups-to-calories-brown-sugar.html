Common questions

How many Calories are in a cup of Brown Sugar?

One cup of Brown Sugar is approximately 836.0 Calories, based on standard density measurements.

Does the conversion change if the Brown Sugar is melted or solid?

This conversion assumes firmly packed brown sugar, which is the standard most recipes intend. Loosely measured brown sugar can weigh 15–20% less per cup.

Is this conversion the same in every country?

Cup measurements vary slightly between US, UK, and metric cup standards. This converter uses the US standard cup (240ml).
