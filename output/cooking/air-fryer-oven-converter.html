Common questions

What is the rule for converting oven recipes to an air fryer?

Reduce the oven temperature by about 25°F (roughly 15-20°C) and reduce the cooking time by about 20%, since air fryers circulate hot air more efficiently than a conventional oven.

Is this conversion exact?

No — it's a starting point. Always check for doneness rather than relying on time alone, and use a food thermometer for meat and poultry.
