Slow Cooker to Oven Converter

Standard reference guidance for converting between oven time and slow cooker settings.

Oven Time (~325°F)Slow Cooker — LowSlow Cooker — High
15–30 min4–6 hours1.5–2.5 hours
35–45 min6–8 hours3–4 hours
50 min – 3 hours8–10 hours4–6 hours
This is general guidance, not an exact formula. Slow cooker models vary in actual temperature output, and food quantity and starting temperature both affect real cooking time. This conversion genuinely isn't linear, which is why it's shown as a reference table rather than a calculator. For meat and poultry, always confirm doneness with a food thermometer — 165°F for poultry, 145°F with a rest for whole cuts, 160°F for ground meat, per USDA guidance — rather than relying on time alone.

Common questions

How long on Low equals High on a slow cooker?

As a general guide, 1 hour on High is roughly equal to 2 to 2.5 hours on Low, though this varies by recipe and slow cooker model.

Is slow cooker timing exact?

No — always confirm doneness with a food thermometer for meat rather than relying on time alone.
